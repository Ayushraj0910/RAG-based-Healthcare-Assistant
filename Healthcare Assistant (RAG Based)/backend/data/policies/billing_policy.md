# Billing and Financial Responsibility Policy

## Financial Responsibility
Patients are responsible for charges not covered by their insurance plan, subject to approved financial-assistance arrangements.

## Uninsured Patients
Patients without insurance should be informed about available self-pay and financial-assistance options. Staff should not deny emergency clinical assessment solely because a patient cannot immediately demonstrate insurance coverage.

## Payment Plans
Eligible patients may request an approved payment plan. The billing department determines eligibility and documents the agreed schedule.

## Billing Questions
Disputes or questions about charges should be routed to the billing department for review. Clinical staff should not alter invoices directly.

## Financial Assistance
Patients may be screened for hospital financial-assistance programs according to the program's eligibility rules and documentation requirements.
