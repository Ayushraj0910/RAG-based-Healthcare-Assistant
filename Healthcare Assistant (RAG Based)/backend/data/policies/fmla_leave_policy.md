# Staff Leave and FMLA Policy

## Requesting Leave
Employees should submit leave requests through the designated HR process as early as practicable and provide required supporting information.

## FMLA Eligibility
Eligibility for protected FMLA leave depends on applicable employment requirements and the employee's qualifying circumstances. HR makes the formal eligibility determination.

## Serious Health Conditions
A qualifying serious health condition may include circumstances defined under applicable FMLA rules. Employees should provide the certification requested by HR.

## Maternity and Parental Leave
Employees should contact HR to determine eligibility, documentation requirements, and the interaction between FMLA and other available leave programs.

## Confidentiality
Medical information supplied for leave administration must be handled confidentially and only by personnel with a legitimate need to access it.
