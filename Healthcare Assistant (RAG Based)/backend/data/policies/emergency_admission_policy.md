# Emergency Admission and Bed Allocation Policy

## Emergency Priority
Patients requiring emergency care are assessed according to clinical urgency. Emergency admission decisions must not be based solely on ability to pay.

## Bed Assignment
When an inpatient bed is required, bed control coordinates assignment using clinical requirements, isolation needs, specialty requirements, and current capacity.

## Overflow
When no appropriate bed is immediately available, the responsible clinical team follows the hospital escalation process and maintains appropriate monitoring until placement is available.

## Documentation
Admission status, bed assignment, transfers, and relevant clinical requirements must be documented in the patient record.

## Infection Control
Patients requiring isolation precautions must be placed in an appropriate room or area according to infection-control requirements and available capacity.
