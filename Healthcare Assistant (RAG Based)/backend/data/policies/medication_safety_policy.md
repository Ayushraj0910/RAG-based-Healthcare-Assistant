# Medication Safety Policy

## Verification
Medication orders should be reviewed using the hospital's medication verification process before administration.

## High-Risk Medications
High-risk medications require the additional verification steps specified by the medication safety program.

## Documentation
Medication administration, omissions, and clinically relevant adverse reactions must be documented in the patient record.

## Error Reporting
Medication errors and near misses should be reported through the hospital's safety reporting process so that appropriate review can occur.

## Patient Identification
Staff must use the approved patient-identification procedure before medication administration.
