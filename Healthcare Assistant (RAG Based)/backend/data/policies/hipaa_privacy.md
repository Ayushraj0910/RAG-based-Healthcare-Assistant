# HIPAA Privacy and Disclosure Policy

## Minimum Necessary Standard
Staff must access or disclose only the minimum patient information reasonably required to perform the authorized task. Curiosity-based access is prohibited.

## Research Disclosure
Patient information may be disclosed for approved research only when the hospital's privacy and research procedures have been satisfied. Where required, authorization or an applicable institutional waiver must be documented before disclosure.

## Patient Consent
Patient records must not be shared with unauthorized people. Staff should verify the recipient and the purpose of disclosure before releasing PHI.

## Breach Reporting
Suspected loss, unauthorized access, or disclosure of PHI must be reported through the hospital privacy incident process as soon as it is identified.

## Access Logging
Use of patient records is logged. Workforce members may access records only when their role and current work assignment require access.
